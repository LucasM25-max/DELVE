# Yard model assets (GDD-03, zone YRD)

Scripted, deterministic glTF-Binary (`.glb`) authoring per the production
plan (doc 04 §pipeline): one module per asset in
`tools/meshes/gltf_lib.py`, built by `tools/meshes/build_all.py`,
metres, +Y up, ground pivot, **no embedded textures** — materials are
assigned in the scene (Godot `StandardMaterial3D` subresources in
`scenes/world/test_yard.tscn`).

`manifest.json` is regenerated by the build and is authoritative for
vertex/triangle counts and per-primitive bounds (glTF local frame).

## glTF → Godot placement

Godot 4.x imports glTF **with identity axes** (verified empirically;
no Z flip). World orientation is therefore set with the instance
transform in the scene:

* **Gatehouse** — authored with the threshold (inner) face at local
  `z=0` and the dock face at `z=+3`; the scene instance uses a 180° Y
  rotation so the block occupies world `z −3..0`, straddling the south
  wall line exactly like its pier collisions (threshold at `z=0`, yard
  side).
* **Gate doors** — pinned open 90° into the passage with EAST/WEST
  bases at `x = ∓/±1.99` (1 cm stand-off from the pier faces; no
  coplanar faces). Bands face the passage centre.
* **Props A1/A2** — authored front-on local +Z; placed rotated 180° so
  the open shutter / parchment face the gate (deviation D3).

## Documented deviations (also in docs/10_YARD_BUILD_REPORT.md)

* **D1 — Portcullis 3.56 m vs 4 m.** The 4 m passage holds both 2 m
  doors pinned against the pier faces; a full-width grid would cut
  through the leaves. 11 bars on 0.32 m centres (x ±1.60), rails to
  ±1.78 — the inset reads as the frame line of the gate slot.
* **D2 — Door 1 cm stand-off.** Leaves sit 1 cm off the pier faces to
  avoid coplanar z-fighting; visually flush.
* **D3 — Guard box / notice board rot 180° vs spec r0.** Spec r0 puts
  the box opening and the board face away from the gate; both are
  rotated so the player arriving through the gate sees the open
  shutter and the readable notices.
* **D4 — Raised portcullis top (y 5.0) meets the vault soffit near the
  edges.** Consequence of the user-confirmed raised state in the 4 m
  high segmental vault (soffit drops to 4.26 m at x ±1.78); the pick
  tips of the end bars just graze the soffit line.
* **D5 — "0.6⌀" barrel read as 0.6 m diameter** (r 0.30 bulge, 0.54
  chimes) × 0.9 m tall.
